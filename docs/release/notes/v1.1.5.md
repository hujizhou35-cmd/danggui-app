# 当归 v1.1.5 预发布 / Danggui v1.1.5 Pre-release

> v1.1.5 以固定来源和失败案例深审 iOS 全功能链，重点加固提醒身份、缺失恢复、时区语义、SQLite 与备份恢复边界。Android 完成共享层回归；iOS 仍只提供源码、Swift 测试、无签名构建与 Simulator 证据。

> Version 1.1.5 audits the complete iOS feature chain against fixed sources and counterexamples, with focused hardening for reminder identity, missing-alarm recovery, time-zone intent, SQLite, backup, and restore. Android receives full shared-layer regression coverage; iOS remains source, Swift tests, unsigned builds, and Simulator evidence.

## 亮点 / Highlights

- iOS AlarmKit 镜像和事务在部分损坏时不再让不完整主文件覆盖完整 LKG；Stop/Snooze 统一校验 reminder、revision 与 session。
- 每条原生闹钟、动作、事件、快照和取消都绑定当前数据库代际；replace 在交换前切换隔离栅栏，但保留非活动代际 LKG，交换失败仍能完整回滚，旧代延迟动作也不会误伤新代。
- 待确认业务动作使用独立的 4096 条无损队列，200 条上限只用于诊断；即使队列满载，Stop、稍后提醒、重启恢复和自动截止也会先进入持久安全终态，不会在打开 App 时补响。
- iOS 15–25 普通通知采用无正文的版本化动作身份，旧通知按钮不能修改后来编辑的新提醒。
- 原生事件 ACK 短暂失败不再阻断同轮状态刷新、容量修复和新 revision 排期；权限结果写库与 replace 恢复共用 FIFO 门禁，避免访问已关闭的旧数据库。
- AlarmKit 快照缺失不再伪造“用户已看到/听到”；15 分钟内采用单次、可审计的保守恢复，过期后记录 missed。
- 初次排期、10/30/60 分钟稍后提醒及系统事件保留 canonical IANA 时区，覆盖 DST 切换。
- 数据库恢复及普通启动改用完整 `integrity_check` + FK，不再让能骗过 `quick_check` 的索引损坏进入
  业务写入；恢复包还须匹配本构建的精确表/列/约束/索引 schema，任何额外 trigger/view 都会在
  live 访问前拒绝，准备后再次核对语义与计数；
  create/replace/merge 串行，ZIP 解压前限条目、大小、类型和 central/local 一致性，读取过程同时防
  `stat` 后增长；候选、安全副本和 journal 采用 `VACUUM INTO`、flush 与故障注入合同。
- 分享/恢复临时明文按精确目录在成功、取消、异常和下次启动清理；iOS 文件保护失败会成为可查询
  的真实 unavailable 状态，而不是只写日志。启动在检查保护策略前先清 stale temp；候选写入失败
  逐项清理，权限、磁盘、flush、close/SQLite 错误保留真实类别，不伪装成格式错误。
- 固定 seed 的 640 步模型测试和两条 XCUITest 覆盖事项/笔记、删除恢复及备份后提醒重建。
- 免费 CI 固定 Xcode 16.4 + iOS 18.5 与 Xcode 26.6 + iOS 26.5，不使用 `macos-latest` 或付费 runner。

- Partial native alarm state can no longer replace a complete last-known-good image; every Stop/Snooze path is bound to reminder, revision, and session.
- Replace restore now rotates a device-local alarm generation in the prepared candidate, while merge preserves it. v1.1.4 databases use a stable legacy generation, portable backups scrub the device marker, and interrupted swaps recover the generation belonging to the database that actually won.
- Native alarms, actions, events, snapshots, and cancellations are fenced by that active generation. Inactive last-known-good records and unacknowledged events survive the prepare window, so rollback remains complete and delayed old-generation operations are harmless.
- A separate 4096-entry lossless business-action outbox protects Stop, Snooze, reboot recovery, and automatic cutoff; the 200-entry cap applies only to diagnostics, and queue pressure cannot turn app launch into catch-up ringing.
- Ordinary iOS 15–25 notification actions carry a private, versioned identity, so delayed buttons cannot mutate a newer edit.
- A transient native-event ACK failure no longer blocks state refresh, capacity repair, or scheduling of the new revision in the same pass. Permission-result writes share the replace-restore FIFO gate instead of racing a closed old database.
- A missing AlarmKit snapshot no longer invents user-visible delivery. Danggui performs one bounded, auditable repair inside 15 minutes and records expiry afterward.
- Initial scheduling, 10/30/60-minute Snooze, and native events preserve canonical IANA time zones across DST.
- Restore and ordinary startup use full `integrity_check` plus foreign-key validation, so index corruption hidden from `quick_check` cannot enter business writes. A restore must also match this build's exact table/column/constraint/index schema; any extra trigger or view is rejected before live access, and semantics/counts are checked again after preparation. Create/replace/merge are serialized; ZIP entry count, size, type, and central/local consistency are bounded before decompression, and streaming reads reject post-`stat` growth. Candidates, safety snapshots, and journals use `VACUUM INTO`, flushes, and fault-injection contracts.
- Share/restore plaintext is cleaned from exact temporary roots after success, cancellation, failure, and next launch. Startup purges stale temp before checking protection; candidate failures clean each artifact independently. Permission, disk, flush, close, and SQLite failures retain their real category instead of masquerading as malformed input. iOS data-protection failure is a queryable unavailable state rather than a log-only success.
- A 640-step seeded model test and two XCUITest scenarios cover task/note workflows, trash restore, and post-backup reminder rebuilding.
- Free CI is fixed to Xcode 16.4 + iOS 18.5 and Xcode 26.6 + iOS 26.5, without `macos-latest` or paid runners.

## 已知限制 / Known limits

- `contract-proven`：本版列出的行为合同、故障注入与 Dart/Kotlin/Swift 自动化通过；不代表所有未列出场景已验证。
- `simulator-proven`：固定 iOS 18.5/26.5 Simulator 上的 RunnerTests、两条代表性 app-process XCUITest 合同与无签名构建通过；通知系统边界使用进程内替身。
- `device-unverified`：真实 iPhone 扬声器、触感、静音/专注、隔夜锁屏、重启、系统强杀及长期后台投递；本轮无实体小米/OEM 测试时也会明确列出。
- 数据库文件交换达到 `process-crash-consistent`，但 Dart 无父目录 fsync；突然断电、磁盘控制器缓存和
  真实磁盘满仍为 `power-loss-unverified`，不得据自动化结果宣称断电安全。

- `contract-proven`: the listed behavior contracts, fault injections, and Dart/Kotlin/Swift automation passed; unlisted scenarios are not implied.
- `simulator-proven`: RunnerTests, two representative app-process XCUITest contracts, and unsigned builds passed on the fixed iOS 18.5/26.5 gates; the notification system boundary is an in-process double.
- `device-unverified`: physical-iPhone sound, haptics, silent/Focus modes, overnight lock screen, reboot, process termination, and long-lived background delivery; physical Xiaomi/OEM testing is also listed when unavailable.
- Database file replacement is `process-crash-consistent`, but Dart cannot fsync the parent directory. Sudden power loss, storage-controller caches, and real disk exhaustion remain `power-loss-unverified`; automation is not evidence of power-loss safety.

不提供 IPA 或 TestFlight，不使用普通 Apple 账号伪装发布验收。历史 v1.1.4 及更早标签、附件和校验值保持不变。

为保证 replace 恢复可以回滚，Android 当前保留跨数据库代际的最后已知良好提醒镜像；频繁执行 replace 恢复可能逐步增加应用私有状态体积，后续版本将在具备数据库提交握手后再安全压缩。该取舍不影响普通事项/提醒数据，也不会把旧代动作应用到当前数据库。

No IPA or TestFlight artifact is provided, and an ordinary Apple account is not presented as release validation. The v1.1.4 and earlier tags, assets, and checksums remain unchanged.

To preserve rollback after a replace restore, Android currently keeps last-known-good alarm projections across database generations. Repeated replace restores may grow private app state until a future database-commit handshake can compact it safely; old-generation actions remain fenced from the active database.
